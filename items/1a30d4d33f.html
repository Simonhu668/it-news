微软 To Do 应用 2027 年 6 月 30 日退役：工作与学校账户转 Planner，个人账户无微软替代品

官方依据：微软官方支持文档，2026 年 11 月 18 日随 Ignite 公告发布｜适用产品及版本：Microsoft To Do 全平台应用（Web、Windows、Mac、iOS、Android），工作与学校账户及个人账户均受影响｜…

support.microsoft.com·官方公告Microsoft 365近期需计划🔵 变更通知收录 11-21 12:30

正文 · AI 翻译

Microsoft To Do 即将退役。对于工作或学校账户，Microsoft Planner 将成为在管理指派工作的同时管理个人工作任务的目的地。更新后的 My tasks 体验将于 2026 年 12 月开始推出。

Microsoft To Do 应用将于 2027 年 6 月 30 日退役，包括 Web、Windows、Mac、iOS 与 Android 上的 To Do，涵盖工作或学校账户与个人 Microsoft 账户。底层的 To Do 服务、API 及相关同步支持将按各自的、更晚的时间表退役，相关日期与所需的准备工作将另行沟通。

个人 Microsoft 账户须知：To Do 应用同样面向个人 Microsoft 账户退役，包括 Microsoft 365 Personal 与 Family 订阅。Planner 不适用于这些账户，请按下方个人账户准备指南操作。同时使用工作与个人账户的，应分别检查各账户中的任务与列表。

Planner 中的新版 My tasks：Planner 现已面向工作或学校账户提供。更新后的 My tasks 体验将于 2026 年 12 月开始推出，便于记录、整理与专注处理个人任务。体验更简洁、以列表为先：记录并管理个人任务，指派的工作可在单独的 Assigned to me 视图中查看；可为任务加星以便聚焦，按自己的方式整理任务；新创建的个人任务将开始存储在 Planner 支持的私人计划中，现有 To Do 任务不会迁移。

现有任务与列表会怎样？现有 To Do 任务与列表不会迁移到 Planner。现有 To Do 任务在 Planner 中可访问至 2027 年 6 月 30 日，此后将无法在 Planner 中查看或管理，数据也不会迁移到新的私人 Planner 计划。应用退役本身不会安排现有任务数据的删除，数据保留与访问是独立事项。

Planner 与 To Do 的差异：Planner 采用不同的任务与协作模式。组织方式上，Planner 将私人任务与指派工作汇入 My tasks，目前提供 Grid 与 Board 视图，新的 List 视图随即将推出的 My tasks 刷新一同到来；提醒方面，Planner 的到期日通知与 To Do 中为单个任务安排的提醒不同；共享与协作方面，Planner 通过计划与任务指派支持共享工作，现有 To Do 共享列表的成员、指派与权限不会转移；桌面与离线访问方面，Planner 提供 Web 与 Teams 体验，未计划推出 Windows 与 Mac 原生 Planner 应用替代 To Do 桌面应用，Web 与 Teams 体验也不提供等效的桌面离线功能；分类与标签方面，Planner 标签与 Outlook 分类的工作方式不同，且不同步；文件附件方面，新 Planner 支持的私人计划中的任务目前不支持文件附件；个人加星与共享优先级方面，个人加星帮助聚焦自己的工作，任务优先级则用于表达共享工作中的重要性，个人加星是即将推出的 My tasks 体验的一部分。To Do 列表分组在 Planner 中没有直接对应物；To Do 打印与邮件列表的等效功能目前未计划推出。

导出 To Do 数据：微软将在应用退役前提供导出 To Do 数据的方式，相关说明、支持的数据、要求与限制确定后将更新到本文。导出保留支持的数据，但不会把任务或列表迁移到 Planner；导出也不意味着内容可以导入 Planner 或其他应用。如组织有保留或合规要求，在删除或重组任务数据前请联系管理员。

准备工作——工作或学校账户：检查重要任务、列表与共享工作流；确认所依赖的功能并对照本文所述差异；向管理员确认可以访问 Planner；确认需要保留的数据，并在公布后查阅导出说明；遵循组织的迁移指导。

准备工作——个人 Microsoft 账户：检查希望保留的任务、列表与共享工作流；选择符合需求的其他任务管理应用；确认希望保留的数据；在公布后查阅导出说明，了解支持的数据、要求与限制。

管理员须知：检查访问与许可，确认受影响用户可以访问 Planner 且拥有符合条件的许可；本次变更无需新的配置，也不引入单独的管理员控制。若组织内禁用了 Planner，受影响用户须启用访问才能将其作为替代品使用。评估工作流与集成，识别依赖 To Do 的共享列表、应用与流程。评估数据需求，评估现有 To Do 数据与新 Planner 任务的保留、保存与合规需求，并在公布后查阅导出说明与要求。向用户说明当前 Planner 体验与即将推出的 My tasks 体验之间的差异。关注 Message Center，查阅推出细节、所需操作以及后续服务变更。

常见问题：是否需要现在开始使用 Planner？可以现在探索 Planner 并了解工作流的变化，更新后的 My tasks 体验将于 2026 年 12 月开始推出。现有任务与列表会自动迁移吗？不会，现有 To Do 任务在 Planner 中可访问至 2027 年 6 月 30 日，此后无法再查看或管理。2027 年 6 月 30 日数据会被删除吗？该日期适用于 To Do 应用以及通过 Planner 访问现有 To Do 任务，本身不会安排现有任务数据的删除，保留与访问是独立事项。个人 Microsoft 账户用户该怎么办？按个人账户准备步骤检查数据、选择其他任务管理应用并准备导出。如何导出 To Do 数据？微软将在应用退役前提供导出方式，说明、支持的数据、要求与限制将添加到本文的导出章节。是否需要额外的 Planner 许可？Planner 访问取决于组织订阅、分配的许可与管理员设置，部分高级功能需要额外许可。什么替代 To Do 桌面应用？Planner 提供 Web 与 Teams 体验，未计划推出 Windows 与 Mac 原生应用。使用 To Do API 或同步的应用会怎样？这些依赖项有各自的退役时间表，集成可能需要变更，请与管理员或集成提供方确认。经典 Outlook 中的任务会怎样？经典 Outlook 的原生任务体验与 Outlook 中托管的 To Do 体验相互独立，将继续工作并与 Exchange 同步。

原文

Microsoft To Do is retiring. For work or school accounts, Microsoft Planner will be the destination for managing personal work tasks alongside assigned work. The updated My tasks experience begins rolling out in December 2026.

Microsoft To Do apps will retire on June 30, 2027. This includes To Do on the web, Windows, Mac, iOS, and Android, for both work or school accounts and personal Microsoft accounts. The underlying To Do service, APIs, and related synchronization support will retire on separate, later timelines. We'll communicate those dates and any required preparation separately.

Note for personal Microsoft accounts: To Do apps are also retiring for personal Microsoft accounts, including Microsoft 365 Personal and Family. Planner is not available for these accounts. Follow the personal-account preparation guidance below. If you use both work and personal accounts, review the tasks and lists in each account separately.

Updated My Tasks in Planner: Planner is available today for work or school accounts. The updated My tasks experience will begin rolling out in December 2026, making it easier to capture, organize, and focus on your private tasks. A simpler, list-first experience: capture and manage your private tasks, with assigned work accessible in the separate Assigned to me view. Focus and organize your work: star tasks to help you focus. Organize tasks to suit how you work. Personal tasks stored directly in a private Planner plan: newly created private tasks in Planner will start to be stored in a Planner-backed private plan. Existing To Do tasks will not be migrated.

What happens to existing tasks and lists? Existing To Do tasks and lists will not migrate to Planner. Existing To Do tasks will remain accessible in Planner through June 30, 2027. After that date, you will no longer be able to view or manage them in Planner. Their data will not be migrated to the new private Planner plan. App retirement does not itself schedule existing task data for deletion. Data retention and access are separate from app retirement.

How Planner differs from To Do: Planner uses a different task and collaboration model. Organizing work: Planner brings private tasks and assigned work into My tasks, which currently offers Grid and Board views. A new List view is coming with the upcoming refresh of My tasks. Reminders: Planner's due-date notifications differ from To Do's reminders that you schedule for an individual task. Sharing and collaboration: Planner supports shared work through plans and task assignments. Existing To Do shared-list membership, assignments, and permissions will not transfer. Desktop and offline access: Planner offers web and Teams experiences. Native Planner apps for Windows and Mac are not planned as replacements for To Do's desktop apps. The web and Teams experiences do not provide equivalent desktop offline functionality. Categories and labels: Planner labels work differently from Outlook categories and do not synchronize with them. File attachments: file attachments are not currently supported on tasks in the new Planner-backed private plan. Personal starring and shared priority: personal starring helps you focus on your own work; task priority communicates importance within shared work. Personal starring is part of the upcoming My tasks experience. To Do list groups do not have a direct equivalent in Planner. Equivalent To Do printing and email-list capabilities are not currently planned for the replacement experience.

Exporting your To Do data: Microsoft will provide a way to export your To Do data before the apps retire. We'll update this article with instructions, supported data, requirements, and limitations when those details are finalized. Export preserves supported data; it does not migrate tasks or lists into Planner. An export does not mean that the content can be imported into Planner or another application. If your organization has retention or compliance requirements, contact your administrator before deleting or reorganizing task data.

Prepare for the change — if you use a work or school account: review your important tasks, lists, and shared workflows; identify the capabilities you depend on and review the differences described in this article; confirm with your administrator that you have access to Planner; identify data you need to preserve and review the export instructions when published; follow your organization's transition guidance. If you use a personal Microsoft account: review the tasks, lists, and shared workflows you want to keep; choose another task-management application that meets your needs; identify data you want to preserve; review the export instructions when published to understand the supported data, requirements, and limitations. If you're an administrator: review access and licensing — confirm that affected users can access Planner and have eligible licenses. No new configuration is required for this change, and it does not introduce separate administrator controls. If Planner is disabled in your organization, affected users will need access enabled to use it as the replacement. Assess workflows and integrations: identify shared lists, applications, and processes that depend on To Do. Review data requirements: assess retention, preservation, and compliance needs for existing To Do data and new Planner tasks. Review the export instructions and requirements when published. Prepare users: explain the differences between today's Planner experience and the upcoming My tasks experience. Follow Message Center: review announcements for rollout details, required actions, and later service changes.

Frequently asked questions: Do I need to start using Planner now? You can explore Planner now and review how your workflows will change. The updated My tasks experience in Planner will begin rolling out in December 2026. Will my existing tasks and lists move automatically? No. Existing To Do tasks and lists will not migrate to Planner. Existing To Do tasks will remain accessible in Planner through June 30, 2027; after that date, you will no longer be able to view or manage them there. Will my data be deleted on June 30, 2027? The June 30 date applies to To Do apps and access to existing To Do tasks through Planner. It does not itself schedule existing task data for deletion. Retention and access are separate considerations. What should I do if I use To Do with a personal Microsoft account? Follow the personal-account preparation steps above to review your data, choose another task-management application, and prepare for export. How will I export my To Do data? Microsoft will provide a way to export your To Do data before the apps retire. Instructions, supported data, requirements, and limitations will be added to the Exporting your To Do data section. Will I need an additional Planner license? Planner access depends on your organization's subscription, your assigned license, and administrator settings. Some premium capabilities require additional licensing. Check with your administrator to understand what is included for you. What will replace the To Do desktop apps? Planner offers web and Teams experiences. Native Planner apps for Windows and Mac are not planned as replacements for the To Do desktop apps. What happens to applications that use To Do APIs or synchronization? These dependencies have separate retirement timelines. Integrations may require changes; review guidance with your administrator or integration provider. What happens to Tasks in classic Outlook? Classic Outlook's native Tasks experience is separate from the To Do experience hosted in Outlook and will continue working and syncing with Exchange.
