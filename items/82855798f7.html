Teams 会议 AI 策略 2026年11月起拆分为两项：MeetingAI 与 TranscriptionWithMeetingAI，旧 Copilot 策略自动映射

Microsoft Teams 正在更新管理员与会议组织者配置会议 AI 的方式：现有 Copilot 会议策略将被两项更具体的策略取代。MeetingAI 控制 Copilot、Facilitator、Intelligent Recap 与其余 AI 功能在会议中是否可用；TranscriptionWithMeetingAI 控制开启会议 AI 时是否自动开始转录。拆分之后，管理员对 AI 是否可用、是否要求转录（或不依赖转录工作）拥有更清晰、更可预测的控制，包括强制关闭 Copilot 的能力；组织者也能在会议选项中更直观地看到这些设置，并在部分场景下直接控制。会议选项同步更新，使 AI 控件更易理解，并对谁可以启用、管理与使用 AI 能力提供更细粒度的控制，包括取代现有 Allow Copilot and Facilitator 选项的新选项：Who can manage AI 取代现有选项，决定谁可以在会议期间开启或关闭 Copilot 与其余 AI 功能；管理员把 Meeting AI 关闭时，该选项会被设为 No one。Who can use AI 更细粒度地控制哪些参会者可以使用 Copilot 与其余 AI 能力。Start Transcription with AI 让组织者选择是否随 AI 一同开始转录，管理员也可通过 Teams 管理中心控制该选项。Intelligent Recap controls 让组织者决定开启 Copilot 时是否生成 Intelligent Recap，包括不创建转录的会议。 上线安排：目标发布（全球）2026年11月中旬开始，预计 2026年12月初完成；正式发布（全球）2026年11月下旬开始，预计 2026年12月初完成。对组织的影响：原 Copilot 策略把 AI 是否可用与是否要求保存转录合并在一项设置里；更新后，这两类行为由两项独立设置表达：Allow AI in meetings 分为 On、Off but organizers can change、Off and organizers cannot change；Start transcription when AI is turned on 分为 On but organizers can change、On and organizers cannot change、Off。为保持行为可预测并避免无效组合：MeetingAI 设为强制关闭时，转录也会关闭；转录重新开启时，MeetingAI 不再处于强制关闭状态。PowerShell 方面，管理员将使用 MeetingAI 与 TranscriptionWithMeetingAI 取代现有 Copilot 策略；新控件启用后，Copilot 策略不可直接更新，尝试更新会返回改用新策略的指引。现有配置的迁移：微软将一次性把每个现有 Copilot 值映射为对应的 MeetingAI 与 TranscriptionWithMeetingAI 值，保留组织当前的有效行为，管理员无需重建策略。例如：Copilot 开启且默认使用保存的转录，映射为 MeetingAI On、TranscriptionWithMeetingAI On but organizers can change；Copilot 开启且要求保存转录，映射为 MeetingAI On、TranscriptionWithMeetingAI On and organizers cannot change；Copilot 可用且无需转录，映射为 MeetingAI On、TranscriptionWithMeetingAI Off；Copilot 关闭但组织者可启用，映射为 MeetingAI Off but organizers can change、TranscriptionWithMeetingAI 按现有转录配置取值；Copilot 强制关闭，映射为 MeetingAI Off and organizers cannot change、TranscriptionWithMeetingAI Off。Transcription 策略继续保留，控制是否允许转录；新的 TranscriptionWithMeetingAI 策略控制开启会议 AI 时是否开始转录。新设置将出现在 Teams 管理中心会议策略的 Recording and transcription 与 Copilot and other AI 区域。迁移无需管理员操作；上线前建议管理员：检查读取或更新 Copilot 会议策略的自动化或脚本，更新为使用 MeetingAI 与 TranscriptionWithMeetingAI；检查引用 Copilot 策略的自定义工具、操作手册或文档；告知 Teams 管理员现有行为将由 Teams 管理中心的两项设置表达；新控件可用后确认映射配置符合预期意图。不使用 PowerShell 或自定义自动化管理 Teams 会议策略的组织无需操作，现有配置将自动映射。合规方面：该变更影响会议中 AI 与转录可用性的管理控制，不改变现有客户数据的处理或存储方式；组织可按需要复核 AI 与转录治理策略。

原文

Microsoft Teams is updating how administrators and meeting organizers configure AI for Teams meetings. The existing Copilot meeting policy is being replaced by two more specific policies: MeetingAI, which controls whether Copilot, Facilitator, Intelligent Recap, and other AI features are available in meetings, and TranscriptionWithMeetingAI, which controls whether transcription starts automatically when meeting AI is turned on. This gives administrators clearer, more predictable control over whether AI is available and whether it requires (or works without) transcription, including the ability to enforce Copilot off. Organizers also gain more visibility into, and in some cases control over, these settings directly from the meeting options. Meeting options are also being updated to make AI controls easier to understand and to provide more granular control over who can enable, manage, and use AI capabilities, including a new who can manage option that replaces the existing Allow Copilot and Facilitator meeting option: Who can manage AI replaces the existing Allow Copilot and Facilitator option and determines who can turn Copilot and other AI features on or off during a meeting. If an admin turns Meeting AI off, this option is set to No one. Who can use AI provides more granular control over which meeting participants can use Copilot and other AI capabilities. Start Transcription with AI lets organizers choose whether transcription starts with AI. Administrators can also control this option through Teams admin center. Intelligent Recap controls lets organizers decide whether an Intelligent Recap is created when Copilot is turned on, including for meetings where a transcript is not created. Rollout schedule: Targeted Release (Worldwide) will begin rolling out in mid-November 2026 and is expected to complete by early December 2026. General Availability (Worldwide) will begin rolling out in late November 2026 and is expected to complete by early December 2026. Impact on your organization: today, the Copilot policy combines whether AI is available in a meeting with whether a saved transcript is required. After this update, these behaviors are represented by two separate settings: Allow AI in meetings (On / Off, but organizers can change / Off, and organizers cannot change) and Start transcription when AI is turned on (On, but organizers can change / On, and organizers cannot change / Off). To keep the experience predictable and avoid invalid combinations: if MeetingAI is set to enforced off, transcription will also be turned off; if transcription is then turned back on, MeetingAI will no longer be enforced off. In PowerShell, administrators will use MeetingAI and TranscriptionWithMeetingAI instead of the existing Copilot policy. Once the new controls are enabled, the Copilot policy can no longer be updated directly; attempts will return guidance to use the new policies. What happens to existing configurations: Microsoft will perform a one-time update mapping each existing Copilot value to the corresponding MeetingAI and TranscriptionWithMeetingAI values, preserving the current effective behavior for your organization. Administrators do not need to recreate their policies. Examples: Copilot is on and uses a saved transcript by default maps to On and On, but organizers can change; Copilot is on and a saved transcript is required maps to On and On, and organizers cannot change; Copilot is available without transcription maps to On and Off; Copilot is off, but organizers can enable it maps to Off, but organizers can change and a value based on the existing transcription configuration; Copilot is enforced off maps to Off, and organizers cannot change and Off. The Transcription policy remains available and continues to control whether transcription is allowed. The new TranscriptionWithMeetingAI policy controls whether transcription starts when meeting AI is turned on. The new settings will appear in the Teams admin center meeting policy under Recording and transcription and Copilot and other AI. No admin action is required to migrate existing configurations. Before rollout, admins are recommended to: review any automation or scripts that read or update the Copilot meeting policy, and update them to use MeetingAI and TranscriptionWithMeetingAI; review custom tooling, runbooks, or documentation referencing the Copilot policy; inform Teams administrators that existing behavior will now be represented by two settings in Teams admin center; confirm the mapped configuration reflects intended behavior once the new controls are available. If you do not use PowerShell or custom automation to manage Teams meeting policies, no action is required. Existing configurations will be mapped automatically. Compliance considerations: this change affects administrative controls over AI and transcription availability in meetings but does not alter how existing customer data is processed or stored. Review AI and transcription governance policies for your organization as appropriate.
