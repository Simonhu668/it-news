SharePoint 数据访问治理新增组权限报告：按组查全站访问，2026 年 11 月底启动推送

官方依据：微软消息中心 MC1540348（2026 年 11 月 5 日发布），经 SharePoint Mentor 指南转述｜适用产品及版本：SharePoint 管理中心的数据访问治理报告，需 SharePoint 高级管理（Sha…

sharepoint-mentor.com·媒体报道Microsoft 365近期需计划收录 11-21 18:30

正文 · AI 翻译

组权限报告（Group Permissions Report）是 SharePoint 管理中心数据访问治理（Data Access Governance，DAG）的一项新报告能力。本文帮助 SharePoint 管理员理解这份新报告的作用、在数据访问治理中的位置、管理上的使用方法，以及组视角的权限可见性对过度共享审查与 Copilot 就绪的意义。

微软宣布，SharePoint 管理中心新增一份数据访问治理报告，管理员可借此识别某个组可访问的全部站点，并了解访问的范围。微软的消息中心公告 MC1540348 称，该功能将于 2026 年 11 月底开始全球推送，预计 2026 年 12 月初完成，包括 GCC、GCC High 与 DoD 环境。

实际变化在于视角的转变：管理员不再从站点或单个用户出发，而是从组出发调查访问。对于成员广泛的组，包括为供应商创建的组，这一视角尤其有用，因为过宽的组访问可能让内容暴露给非预期接收者。微软明确把这份报告定位为调查潜在暴露、核查组权限、在分配 Copilot 许可前审查权限的工具。

关键结论：把组权限报告理解为以组为中心的访问调查工具——“如果以这个组为审查对象，它能访问哪些 SharePoint 站点，访问范围有多大？”

为什么这份报告重要：SharePoint 权限常通过组授予，因为组授权比逐人直接授权更容易管理。微软的指导建议遵循最小权限、使用标准组。治理难题在于，组成员关系与站点访问会各自随时间演变：一个组在创建时权限适当，之后可能同时获得更广的成员与更广的站点权限。组视角让管理员可以按组调查访问足迹，而不必逐站检查；有助于发现成员广泛的组访问了值得复核的内容；为数据访问治理补充了新的视角，与租户级站点权限基线与以用户为中心的权限报告互补；微软明确建议在分配 Copilot 许可前用它审查组权限。

什么是数据访问治理：数据访问治理是 SharePoint 管理中心的一项报告能力，用于帮助组织治理 SharePoint 数据访问。微软将其描述为发现可能过度共享或含敏感内容的站点、评估暴露情况、应用安全与合规策略的手段。管理员在 SharePoint 管理中心展开“报告”、选择“数据访问治理”即可进入。微软目前提供快照报告与活动报告：快照报告记录生成时的组织状态，活动报告关注潜在的过度共享活动。

新报告的作用：根据微软消息中心公告，新报告允许管理员识别某个组可访问的全部站点及访问范围，目的是更容易调查基于组的暴露、核查组权限是否仍然适当。

推送与可用性：MC1540348 于 2026 年 11 月 5 日发布。公告称，全球、GCC、GCC High 与 DoD 的正式版推送计划于 2026 年 11 月底开始、2026 年 12 月初完成。截至 2026 年 11 月 10 日，推送尚未开始，该功能可能尚未在租户中可见。

前提与管理员访问：微软的数据访问治理文档要求满足 SharePoint 高级管理的前提条件。已公布的数据访问治理入口需要 SharePoint 管理员凭据。不同数据访问治理能力的许可与角色要求可能不同，管理员应在新组报告出现在租户中时验证当前前提条件。许可背景：微软说明数据访问治理属于 SharePoint 高级管理功能集；微软同时说明，持有 Microsoft 365 E5 的 IT 管理员有专门的数据访问治理报告访问权限，但与完整的高级管理体验相比存在限制；在依赖新报告开展工作前，应先验证适用租户的授权。

如何使用组权限报告：微软已公布报告的用途与在数据访问治理中的位置，但现有来源尚未记录新组体验的完整生产环境点击路径。已确认的导航是：使用适当的 SharePoint 管理员凭据登录 SharePoint 管理中心；在左侧窗格展开“报告”；选择“数据访问治理”；推送到达租户后，在该页查找新提供的组权限报告选项。报告可用后的建议审查流程：先选定值得审查的组，例如成员广泛或用于供应商访问的组；生成或打开组视角报告，识别该组可访问的站点；审查每个返回站点的访问范围，标记超出组业务用途的访问；优先调查含敏感、机密、受监管或业务关键内容的站点，必要时结合其他数据访问治理报告；与站点负责人或信息负责人确认该组是否仍需这些访问；对不再合理的访问，按既定权限治理流程应用最小权限修正；记录审查的组、调查的站点、发现、负责人决定与修正结果，使报告成为可重复治理流程的一部分。

报告的价值：把问题从“谁能访问这个站点？”改为“这个组能去哪里？”；支持过度共享调查——微软直接把该功能与防止内容意外暴露联系起来；强化最小权限审查——跨站点的组视图为判断组访问是否仍符合当前业务用途提供了实际证据；支持 Copilot 就绪——微软消息中心指导明确指出，管理员可在分配 Copilot 许可前用新报告审查组权限；有助于建立可重复的治理流程——报告可作为定期访问审查流程的证据来源。

尚未明确的内容：由于该功能在指南编写前仅公告数日、推送尚未开始，若干实施细节在已查阅来源中没有明确记录：每个界面控件的最终生产名称、支持的具体组类型、每个访问字段的详细输出列与定义、报告生成时长、刷新频率、保留期、行数限制与导出细节、新报告是否内置筛选、直接修正、站点访问审查或 AI 洞察功能、针对组报告的 PowerShell 语法。不要从其他数据访问治理报告推断这些细节，应在功能到达租户后以微软文档或 SharePoint 管理中心验证。

管理员清单：确认功能已到达租户；确认适用的 SharePoint 高级管理／数据访问治理许可与角色；确定优先审查的组；运行组视角的访问调查；审查可访问站点与显示的访问范围；必要时用其他数据访问治理信号交叉核查高风险站点；与负责人确认业务需求；通过既定权限控制实施最小权限修正；按治理流程记录决定并保留证据；当组用途、成员、外部协作或 AI 部署发生变化时重复审查。

THE GROUP PERMISSIONS REPORT A practical guide to the new Data Access Governance reporting capability

Purpose

Help SharePoint administrators understand what the new Group Permissions Report is designed to do, where it fits within Data Access Governance, how administrators will use it, and why group-centric permission visibility matters for oversharing reviews and Copilot readiness.

Executive summary

Microsoft has announced a new Data Access Governance report in the SharePoint admin center that is designed to let SharePoint administrators identify all sites accessible to a given group and understand the extent of that group's access. Microsoft's Message Center announcement, MC1540348, says the feature will begin worldwide rollout in late November 2026 and is expected to complete by early December 2026, including GCC, GCC High, and DoD environments.

The practical shift is important: instead of starting with a site or an individual user, an administrator can investigate access from the perspective of a group. This is especially useful for groups with broad membership, including groups created for vendors, because overly broad group access can expose content to unintended recipients. Microsoft specifically positions the report as a way to investigate potential exposure, verify group permissions, and review permissions before assigning Copilot licenses.

Key takeaway

Think of the Group Permissions Report as a group-centered access investigation tool: "If this group is the subject of my review, which SharePoint sites can it reach, and what is the extent of that access?"

1. Why this report matters

SharePoint permissions are often granted through groups because group-based access is easier to manage than direct permission assignments to many individual users. Microsoft guidance recommends following least privilege and using standard groups where appropriate. The governance problem is that group membership and site access can evolve independently over time. A group that was appropriate when first created may later acquire broader membership, broader site permissions, or both.

- Group-centric visibility enables an administrator to investigate the access footprint of a specific group across sites rather than checking sites one at a time. - It can help expose situations where a broadly populated group has access to content that warrants closer review. - It adds another perspective to Data Access Governance, complementing tenant-wide site permission baselines and user-centric permission reports. - Microsoft specifically recommends this capability for reviewing group permissions before assigning Copilot licenses.

2. What is Data Access Governance?

Data Access Governance (DAG) is a reporting capability in the SharePoint admin center intended to help organizations govern access to SharePoint data. Microsoft describes DAG reports as a way to discover sites that may contain overshared or sensitive content, assess exposure, and apply appropriate security and compliance policies.

Administrators access the DAG area in the SharePoint admin center by expanding Reports and selecting Data access governance. Microsoft currently documents snapshot reports and activity reports. Snapshot reports capture the organization's status at the time a report is generated; activity reports focus on potential oversharing activity.

3. What the new Group Permissions Report does

According to Microsoft's Message Center announcement, the new report allows SharePoint administrators to identify all sites accessible to a given group and the extent of that access. Its purpose is to make it easier to investigate group-based exposure and verify whether a group's SharePoint permissions remain appropriate.

4. Rollout and availability

MC1540348 was published November 5, 2026. The announcement states that General Availability rollout for Worldwide, GCC, GCC High, and DoD is planned to begin in late November 2026 and complete by early December 2026. Because today is November 10, 2026, the capability may not yet be visible in a tenant.

5. Prerequisites and administrator access

Microsoft's Data Access Governance documentation directs organizations to the prerequisites for SharePoint Advanced Management. The documented DAG entry point requires SharePoint administrator credentials. Licensing and role requirements can vary by DAG capability, so administrators should validate the current prerequisites for the new group report when it appears in their tenant.

Licensing context: Microsoft documents that Data Access Governance is part of the SharePoint Advanced Management feature set. Microsoft also notes special Data Access Governance reporting access for IT administrators with Microsoft 365 E5, with limitations compared with the full SharePoint Advanced Management experience. Validate the entitlement that applies to your tenant before relying on the new report operationally.

6. How to use the Group Permissions Report

Microsoft has announced the report's purpose and Data Access Governance location, but the sources available for this guide do not yet document a complete production click-path for the new group-specific experience.

Confirmed navigation into Data Access Governance: 1. Sign in to the SharePoint admin center with appropriate SharePoint administrator credentials. 2. Open Reports: in the left pane, expand Reports. 3. Open Data access governance: select Data access governance. When rollout reaches your tenant, look for the newly available group-permissions reporting option on this page.

Recommended review workflow after the report is available: 1. Choose the group to investigate. Start with a group that warrants review, for example a group with broad membership or one used for vendor access. 2. Generate or open the group-focused report. Use the new report to identify the sites accessible to the selected group. 3. Review the extent of access. Examine the access information Microsoft exposes for each returned site and flag access that appears broader than the group's business purpose. 4. Investigate high-risk relationships. Prioritize sites containing sensitive, confidential, regulated, or business-critical content, using other DAG reports and your governance context where appropriate. 5. Validate business need. Confirm with site owners or information owners whether the group still requires the access it holds. 6. Remediate through your normal permission-governance process. Where access is no longer justified, use established SharePoint permission-management practices and least privilege. 7. Record the review. Capture the group reviewed, sites investigated, findings, owner decisions, and remediation outcome so the report becomes part of a repeatable governance process.

7. The value of the Group Permissions Report

A. It changes the question from "who can access this site?" to "where can this group go?" B. It supports oversharing investigations: Microsoft directly links the feature to preventing unintended content exposure. Groups with broad membership, including vendor groups, can create risk when their access extends beyond their intended collaboration boundary. C. It strengthens least-privilege reviews: Microsoft SharePoint permission guidance recommends least privilege. A cross-site group view gives administrators a practical evidence set for asking whether a group's access still matches its current business purpose. D. It supports Copilot readiness: Microsoft's Message Center guidance explicitly says administrators can use the new report to review group permissions before assigning Copilot licenses. E. It helps establish repeatable governance: the report can serve as an evidence source in a recurring access-review process.

8. What the available sources do not yet specify

Because this feature was announced only days before this guide was prepared and its rollout has not yet begun, several implementation details are not explicitly documented in the sources reviewed: the final production name of every UI control in the new report experience; the exact group types supported; the detailed output columns and definitions for each access field; report generation duration, refresh cadence, retention, row limits, and export details specific to the new group report; whether the final UI includes built-in filtering, direct remediation, Site Access Review, or AI Insights for this specific report; PowerShell syntax specifically for the group-focused report. Do not assume these details from other DAG reports. Validate them in Microsoft documentation or in the SharePoint admin center once the feature reaches your tenant.

9. Administrator checklist

- Confirm the feature has reached your tenant. - Confirm applicable SharePoint Advanced Management / Data Access Governance licensing and roles. - Identify high-priority groups for review. - Run the group-focused access investigation. - Review accessible sites and the extent of access shown by the report. - Cross-check high-risk sites using other DAG signals where useful. - Validate business need with the responsible owner. - Apply least-privilege remediation through established permission controls. - Document decisions and retain evidence according to your governance process. - Repeat reviews when group purpose, membership, external collaboration, or AI deployment changes.

Sources: Microsoft Learn: Data access governance reports for SharePoint and OneDrive sites; Microsoft Learn: Get your organization's site permissions baseline with the snapshot report; Microsoft Learn: What is SharePoint Advanced Management?; Microsoft 365 Message Center item MC1540348 (announcement summarized by public archive), published November 5, 2026; rollout announced for late November through early December 2026.
