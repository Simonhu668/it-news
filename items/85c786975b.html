Windows 11 26H2 把设置备份改为默认开启，IT 需审计策略

26H2 起 Windows 设置备份与还原改为默认开启，企业设备可能在没有明显提示的情况下开始把数据备份到云端；建议现在就到 Intune/MDM 里确认备份还原策略状态，决定是顺其自然还是显式关闭，别等合规审计时才发现。

正文 · AI 翻译

Windows 11 26H2 带来了一个让企业管理员意外的变化：微软将设置备份改为对符合条件的设备默认开启。过去需要明确选择加入，现在除非被阻止否则自动启用。

该变化影响加入 Entra 或混合加入、运行新功能更新的系统。备份策略保持「未配置」的组织将看到用户设置和 Microsoft Store 应用列表自动上传到云端，备份大约每 8 天进行一次。微软在消息中心更新（MC1483538）中表示：「如果用户设备需要恢复，您不再需要猜测其 Windows 设置是否有备份。默认开启后，用户在设备重置、更换或升级后更可能找回设置和 Microsoft Store 应用列表。」恢复功能仍默认关闭，管理员需单独启用。

该功能原名 Windows Backup for Organizations，2024 年 11 月在 Ignite 上首次亮相，2025 年 5 月进入公开预览，2025 年 8 月正式可用。7 月的公告首次预告了与 26H2 绑定的默认变更；随着 2026 年 9 月 29 日更新发布，该策略现已在符合条件的机器上激活。原本在 26H1 的设备将在下一次功能更新后启用。

适用范围有明确限制：仅适用于 Windows 11 26H2 及更高版本，且不在欧盟《数字市场法》监管区域内；主权云和受限环境豁免，隐私敏感国家不会自动激活。明确设置的策略（无论启用还是禁用）优先于一切。

管理员可通过 Intune 设置目录策略、组策略（管理模板 > 同步你的设置）或 MDM 工具控制。关键开关「Enable Windows Backup」可锁定关闭该行为。除非被组织策略限制，用户仍可通过 Windows 备份应用或设置页面单独关闭备份。

原文

Windows 11 version 26H2 arrived with an unexpected shift for enterprise administrators: Microsoft turned on settings backup by default for eligible devices. What once required explicit opt-in now activates unless blocked.

The change affects Entra-joined and hybrid-joined systems running the new feature update. Organizations that left the backup policy at "Not Configured" will see automatic uploads of user settings and Microsoft Store app lists to the cloud. Backups occur roughly every eight days. "If user devices ever enter recovery, you don't have to guess whether their Windows settings have a backup," Microsoft stated in its Message Center update (MC1483538). "With this feature on by default, users are more likely to have their settings and Microsoft Store app list available to restore after a device reset, replacement, or upgrade." Restore stays off by default; administrators must still enable it separately.

The feature, once called Windows Backup for Organizations, debuted at Microsoft Ignite in November 2024, entered public preview in May 2025 and reached general availability in August 2025. July announcements first signaled the default change tied to 26H2; with the update's release on September 29, 2026, the policy now activates on qualifying machines. Devices originally on 26H1 pick it up after their next feature update.

Eligibility comes with clear limits: the default applies only to devices on Windows 11 26H2 or later, outside regions regulated by the EU Digital Markets Act. Sovereign clouds and restricted environments stay exempt, and explicit policy settings — whether enabled or disabled — override everything.

Admins retain control through Intune Settings catalog policies, Group Policy under Administrative Templates for Sync your settings, or MDM tools. The key toggle, "Enable Windows Backup," can lock the behavior off. Users can still toggle backup off individually via the Windows Backup app or Settings page, unless restricted by organizational rules.
