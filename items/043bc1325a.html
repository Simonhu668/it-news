正文 · AI 翻译

2026 年 11 月 12 日，Microsoft 365 不再像一组产品那样运行，而是像一个系统那样一起出故障。微软服务健康更新的公开时间线记录：美洲地区 Teams 事故从 14:45 UTC 持续至 15:53 UTC；Exchange Online 邮箱访问从 16:17 UTC 起失败，编号 EX1550543；18:26 UTC，为所有依赖 Exchange Online 的服务开启了第二个事故 MO1550821。当晚，Intune、Defender for Office 365 与 Purview 也加入受影响名单。11 月 13 日 17:57 UTC，微软称事故由“一次早期网络容量升级中的问题”引入，并报告“广泛恢复与稳定”。11 月 14 日 06:09 UTC，微软称已“为其余受影响客户和区域恢复服务”。其事故记录现在写着“服务已恢复”，影响窗口为 11 月 12 日 10:05 UTC 至 11 月 13 日 16:10 UTC。截至 11 月 15 日 03:28 UTC，微软尚未发布事后复盘，也未公布受影响租户数量。

Greyhound Research 的立场是：集中在一个套件上，对大多数企业而言仍是正确策略，但围绕它的标准条款已经不够。三件事决定买方的风险敞口，而这三件都不随许可证附带：对厂商变更关口的了解、在恢复与通知顺序中的位置、事先写好并演练过的兜底方案。

支撑论证的三项发现：回顾自 2023 年 1 月以来的十起多服务事故，微软自己的说法将其中六起归因于其所做的变更，另有两起归因于外部触发背后的自身配置；澳大利亚政府称其启动了一项与微软的非约束性协议以确保恢复优先权，这让恢复顺序成为客户可能有、也可能没有书面约定的东西；五家套件的评分卡显示，微软就断服问题写下的内容最多，但没有任何一家厂商写明恢复顺序。

这份研究记录给出了首席信息官与首席信息安全官的十个动作、面向更广泛领导层的十步行动手册、八项谈判条款、七个董事会问题和十个要问微软的问题。其中没有一项买方行动需要等厂商。最先的三项是：在 30 天内导出事故记录、在 2026 年 12 月 31 日前提交服务积分索赔、为每个不能停的流程写一页并演练。

证据的局限：微软的声明有据可查，但其原因说法目前只有一句话，尚待复盘；服务等级条款为微软 2026 年 10 月标准版，谈判协议可能修改；关于恢复顺序的解读，依据的是一位部长的声明与一个有两种解释的恢复次序。Greyhound Research 表示，将在微软的复盘发布后修订相关结论。

原文

On November 12, 2026, Microsoft 365 failed as one system. Mailbox access in Exchange Online failed from 16:17 UTC, a wider incident was opened at 18:26 UTC for every service that depends on it, and security and management services, including Defender, Intune, and Purview, joined the affected list that evening. On November 13 Microsoft said the incident was introduced by an issue during an earlier network capacity upgrade. A day later it said it had restored service to the remaining affected customers and regions. At 03:28 UTC on November 15, no post-incident review and no count of affected customers had been published.

Greyhound Research's position is that consolidation on one suite remains the right strategy for most enterprises, and that the standard terms around it are no longer enough. Three things decide a buyer's exposure, and none comes with the license: knowledge of the vendor's change gate, a place in the order of restoration and notification, and fallbacks the buyer has written down and rehearsed.

Three findings carry the argument. In a review of ten multi-service incidents since January 2023, Microsoft's own account attributes six to a change it made and two more to its own configuration behind an outside trigger. Australia's government said it activated a non-binding agreement with Microsoft to secure priority in recovery, which made the order of restoration visible as something a customer may or may not hold in writing. And a scorecard of five suites shows Microsoft has put the most in writing about outages, while no vendor states an order of restoration.

The research note sets out ten moves for chief information officers and chief information security officers, a ten-move Greyhound CXO Runbook for the wider leadership, eight negotiation terms, seven boardroom questions, and ten questions for Microsoft. None of the buyer actions waits on the vendor. The first three are to export the incident record before it ages out after 30 days, to file the credit claim by December 31, 2026, and to write and rehearse one page for each process that cannot stop.

The evidence has limits. Microsoft's statements are documented, and its cause rests on a single sentence ahead of any review. The service level terms are Microsoft's standard edition of October 2026, which a negotiated agreement may amend, and the reading of restoration order rests on one minister's statement and a recovery sequence with two explanations.
