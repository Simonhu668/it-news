策略设置可能产生的影响；告知用户该变化并更新内部文档。微软称上线自动进行，事前无需操作；但该功能默认关闭，因此以 Entra ID 登录的用户不会突然发现 Copilot 在读取全部标签。治理方面，如果 Copilot 被 DLP 或 EdgeEntraCopilotPageContext 策略阻止访问页面内容，上下文访问会被压制，DLP 保持有效；Purview 端点 DLP、Intune 原文

Microsoft has marked roadmap item 499423 as launched. The item lets Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat in Edge for Business answer questions about the browser tabs you have open and the YouTube videos you are watching, all from the Edge side pane. The roadmap lists general availability as October 2026 on the web, for the Worldwide (Standard Multi-Tenant) cloud. The part worth watching is what the latest version of the related admin notice no longer says. For almost a year, Microsoft described a bigger package that also covered Word, Excel and PowerPoint documents. The October 30 version of that notice mentions only tabs and YouTube. The related Message Center post is MC1187682. Microsoft says the feature enables users to ask Copilot questions about multiple open browser tabs, and YouTube videos directly from the Edge side pane. The notice lists the rollout status as General Availability (Multiple browser tabs and YouTube videos): Currently Available. In practice, you can open a vendor proposal, a competitor's pricing page and an internal wiki article in three tabs and ask Copilot to compare them. You can also ask what a 45-minute conference keynote said about licensing without watching all of it. Copilot answering questions about your Office files in the browser has had a long and shifting rollout. The first version of MC1187682, published in November 2025, said Edge for Business would support Copilot summarize and provide context from browser tabs, Microsoft 365 documents, and YouTube videos starting mid-November 2025. The schedule then moved several times: later update, starting mid-March 2026; August 2026 update, rollout expected to begin mid-September 2026; September 2026 update, rollout expected to begin mid-October 2026; October 30, 2026 update, not mentioned; only tabs and YouTube are listed. Microsoft has not said the Office-document part is cancelled, and you should not read that into it. It may come back under another roadmap ID or a later notice. But anyone who promised users 'Copilot reads your Word docs in Edge' based on earlier versions of MC1187682 should check that claim again. Microsoft Learn's page on what Copilot can read in Edge for Business still lists Office Documents as unsupported for page-content access. The audience is the same as before: users signed in to Microsoft Edge for Business with their Entra ID and who have access to Microsoft 365 Copilot. Microsoft's preparation checklist for admins: ensure users who need this capability have access to Microsoft 365 Copilot; ensure your tenant is configured to support Microsoft 365 Copilot and that eligible users meet all licensing prerequisites, including Entra ID sign-in and applicable Microsoft 365 service plans; review current DLP and Edge policy settings for possible impact; tell users about the change and update internal documentation. Microsoft says the rollout is automatic and nothing has to be done beforehand. But the feature is off by default. DLP is what makes this usable in a business. Microsoft says that if Copilot is blocked from page content by Data Loss Prevention or the EdgeEntraCopilotPageContext policy, contextual access is suppressed so DLP stays intact. Admins can manage access to Copilot's contextual grounding capabilities via DLP settings and Edge policy configurations, which can be scoped using Entra ID group membership. When a user agrees to share page information, Microsoft Learn says Edge sends Copilot Chat the session's browsing context, the prompt and earlier conversation history. Enterprise data protection covers prompts and responses for Entra-signed-in users. Multi-tab grounding means one prompt can pull from several tabs. If one tab is a public news story and another is an internal HR portal, both can feed the answer unless DLP blocks it. A sensible plan is to check licensing, review DLP coverage of internal domains, pilot with an Entra group, and tell users exactly what works now: open tabs and YouTube videos, not your Word documents.
