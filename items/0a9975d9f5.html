Dynamics 365 Business Central 制造功能全景：原生覆盖到哪、哪些缺口须靠插件补

独立选型指南 2026 年 11 月 2 日梳理 Dynamics 365 Business Central 的制造能力：多级生产 BOM、工艺路线、工作与机加工中心、生产订单、MPS 与 MRP、粗能力负荷均须 Premium 许可（美国…

正文 · AI 翻译

Dynamics 365 Business Central 的制造功能覆盖中小型工厂离散生产的核心：多级生产 BOM、工艺路线、工作与机加工中心、生产订单、MPS 与 MRP、粗能力负荷。以上全部须 Premium 许可，微软美国定价为 110 美元/用户/月，Essentials 为 80 美元/用户/月。不足之处在于精细排程、车间数据采集、产品配置与流程制造，多数制造企业用独立软件供应商（ISV）的插件补齐这些缺口。关键结论：生产 BOM、工艺路线、生产订单与产能计划仅限 Premium；只做套件组装、无工艺路线时，Essentials 的装配管理可能已够；微软文档将原生有限负荷描述为粗排程，不按优先级或优化规则自动生成详细计划；微软在 2026 发布波 1 新增原生质量管理扩展并扩大了分包，缩小了以往须靠插件补的两个缺口；精细排程、车间工时采集、产品配置与食品或流程功能通常仍须另购插件；除 Premium 许可外，还须为插件订阅与合作伙伴实施做预算，并要求书面实施报价。本指南面向约 10 至 250 名员工的美国制造企业，他们正评估 Business Central 作为从 QuickBooks 或独立 MRP 工具升级、或替代 Dynamics NAV 或 GP 的选择；研究方法为阅读微软 Learn 的制造、计划、分包与质量管理文章并记录各页面日期，于 2026 年 10 月 12 日核对美国公开价目，插件价格取自 ISV 产品页面。许可规则：微软制造概览称所述功能仅限 Premium；容易被买家忽略的规则是层级在公司内部的相互作用——Essentials 许可用户无法登录体验字段设为 Premium 的公司，而 Premium 用户可以登录 Essentials 公司但无法使用其中的 Premium 功能。实际操作中，如果生产运行在该公司内，公司内每位正式用户都需要 Premium，包括应付账款文员与销售人员。共享车间终端可用设备许可，多人共用一台设备，并发登录数受设备许可数量限制；美国定价页未列出设备价格，须向合作伙伴询价。装配与生产是两种制造方式：装配 BOM 适用于“资源占用很少或没有”的父项，如套件；生产 BOM 涉及更复杂的工序，如资源使用、生产工艺路线与工作或机加工中心。生产 BOM 列出每个父项的组件数量、可选报废率与可选工艺路线链接码；BOM 经过新建、开发中、已认证状态，必须认证后才能用于生产或计划；版本带有生效日期，新版本到日期后自动接管。工艺路线按串行或并行定义工序，每道工序带有准备时间、运行时间、等待时间与移动时间；产能按工作中心组、工作中心与机加工中心的层级组织，各自挂接车间日历；工序时长按运行时间除以效率再除以产能计算，例如 2 小时运行、80% 效率对应 2.5 小时。生产订单经历模拟、已计划、已确认计划、已下达、已完成状态；计划工作表运行 MPS、MRP 或两者，建议采购、转移、装配与生产订单；计划按净变化工作，标记自上次计划以来需求、供应或计划主数据发生变化的物料。默认按无限产能排程；可将瓶颈工作或机加工中心标记为产能受限资源，设置关键负荷百分比，让工序在可用时段内拆分。微软直言结果是“粗排程”，不会基于优先级或优化规则自动创建并维护详细计划。分包与质量是 2026 年缩小的两个缺口：微软现发布分包扩展，增加分包工作表、直接从已下达生产订单工艺路线创建采购订单、向分包商转移组件与分包商定价；质量管理在 2026 发布波 1 作为微软发布的扩展引入，可从采购收货、生产与装配产出、仓储移动创建检验，使用带通过与不通过标准的模板，并可拦截不合格批次。仍存在的缺口：精细排程——标准产品没有拖拽式排程板，若交期取决于共享瓶颈的排序，须准备插件；车间数据采集——原生过账通过产出、消耗与产能日记账，未描述打卡终端或 MES，实时人工与机器数据通常来自插件或独立 MES；产品配置——制造概览未描述基于规则的产品配置器；流程与食品制造——生产 BOM 面向离散件，Aptean 的流程制造插件补充批量、合并拣货与联产品消耗分配，计重为单独插件；维护与工程变更——概览未描述维护模块，BOM 与工艺路线变更通过状态与版本控制，而非基于审批的变更单流程。原生功能与常见插件对照：多级 BOM 与工艺路线原生支持；MPS 与 MRP 原生支持，Insight Works 增强计划包为 138 美元/公司/月；精细排序排程仅粗排程，Insight Works MxAPS 起价 170.20 美元/地点/月，NETRONIC 可视化生产排程未公开价格；车间工时与产出采集原生只有日记账，Insight Works Shop Floor Insight 低至 3.31 美元/车间员工/月；产品配置器原生未描述，Insight Works 产品配置器为 276 美元/公司/月；质量检验原生支持（2026 年扩展），Insight Works 质量检验器为 184 美元/公司/月；分包原生支持，通常无须插件；批量、联产品、计重能力有限，Aptean 插件价格未公开。成本方面：可从公开页面预算的微软价格只有 Premium 许可，实施由合作伙伴报价；ERP Research 估计 Business Central 的典型三年总成本为 75,000 至 400,000 美元，实施时间为 2 至 6 个月。算例（示意，非报价）：40 人金属加工厂，9 名办公室用户须正式许可，25 人在车间，许可与插件小计每年 17,234.40 美元，未含车间采集、设备许可与实施。独立结论：Business Central 是可信的中小离散制造 ERP，财务、库存与生产集于一体且价格公开；原生 BOM、工艺路线与 MRP 功能成熟，2026 年的质量与分包新增减少了所需插件。评估时须注意两点：Premium 要求适用于制造公司内的每位正式用户，应按全部用户数计价；要求每家合作伙伴先用标准产品演示实际瓶颈排程，再演示拟议插件的效果。三种买家场景：15 人套件与轻装配企业从 Essentials 起步，仅在需要工作中心产能或多级生产时升级 Premium；60 人瓶颈单元作业车间须 Premium，从一开始就为精细排程插件与车间采集工具做预算，并用同一排程场景横向比较；120 人食品或化工批量生产企业通常需要 Aptean 等行业插件，应将总成本与流程型 ERP 及 NetSuite 比较。若只转售成品、全部生产外包，或组装套件而无工艺路线，可能不需要制造功能，Essentials 已覆盖，每用户每月便宜 30 美元。

原文

Dynamics 365 Business Central manufacturing covers the core of discrete production for small and mid-sized plants: multi-level production BOMs, routings, work and machine centers, production orders, MPS and MRP, and rough-cut finite loading. All of it requires the Premium license, which Microsoft lists at $110 per user per month against $80 for Essentials. Where it falls short is detailed scheduling, shop floor data capture, product configuration and process manufacturing, and most manufacturers close those gaps with add-on apps from independent software vendors (ISVs). Key takeaways: production BOMs, routings, production orders and capacity planning are available only with the Premium experience; if you only kit or assemble components without routings, the Assembly Management features available in Essentials may be enough; Microsoft's own documentation describes native finite loading as a rough-cut schedule that does not build detailed schedules from priorities or optimization rules; Microsoft added a native Quality Management extension in 2026 release wave 1 and expanded subcontracting the same year; detailed scheduling, shop floor time capture, product configuration and food or process features still usually come from ISV apps, priced separately; budget for add-on subscriptions and partner implementation on top of the Premium license, and get the implementation quote in writing. This guide is for owners, operations managers and controllers at US manufacturers with roughly 10 to 250 employees who are evaluating Business Central as a move up from QuickBooks or a standalone MRP tool, or as a replacement for Dynamics NAV or GP. Licensing: Microsoft's manufacturing overview states that the features it describes are available only with the Premium experience. Microsoft's user setup documentation says users with an Essentials license can't sign in to a company where the Experience field is set to Premium, while a Premium user can sign in to an Essentials company but cannot use Premium features there. In practice, if production runs in your company, every full user in that company needs Premium, including AP clerks and salespeople. For shared shop floor terminals, a Device license lets several people use one device. Assembly BOMs are for parent items built from components with little to no resource use, such as kits, while production BOMs involve more complex procedures, such as resource usage, production routing, and work or machine centers. Production BOMs move through New, Under Development and Certified statuses and must be certified before they can be used in production or planning; versions carry a starting date. Routings define operations in serial or parallel order; each operation carries setup time, run time, wait time and move time; capacity sits in a hierarchy of work center groups, work centers and machine centers, each tied to a shop calendar. Production orders move through Simulated, Planned, Firm Planned, Released and Finished statuses. The planning worksheet runs MPS, MRP or both and suggests purchase, transfer, assembly and production orders; planning works on net change. By default Business Central schedules against infinite capacity; you can mark bottleneck work or machine centers as capacity constrained resources and set a critical load percentage. Microsoft is direct about the limit: the result is a rough-cut schedule, and Business Central doesn't automatically create and maintain detailed schedules based on priorities or optimization rules. Subcontracting and quality: Microsoft now publishes a Subcontracting extension that adds a subcontracting worksheet, purchase orders created directly from released production order routings, component transfers to the subcontractor, and subcontractor pricing. Quality Management was introduced in 2026 release wave 1 as a Microsoft-published extension; it creates inspections from purchase receipts, production and assembly output and warehouse movements, uses templates with pass and fail criteria, and can block noncompliant lots. Where Business Central falls short: detailed scheduling - no drag-and-drop scheduling board in the standard product; shop floor data capture - native posting runs through output, consumption and capacity journals, with no clock-in terminal or MES described; product configuration - no rules-based configurator described; process and food manufacturing - production BOMs are built for discrete parts, Aptean's Process Manufacturing app adds batch sizing, consolidated picking and co-product consumption allocation; maintenance and engineering change - no maintenance module, and BOM and routing changes are controlled through status and versions rather than an approval-based change order workflow. Native features versus common add-ons: multi-level BOMs and routings - native; MPS and MRP - native, Insight Works Enhanced Planning Pack $138.00 per company per month; finite, sequenced scheduling - rough-cut only, Insight Works MxAPS from $170.20 per location per month, NETRONIC Visual Production Scheduler price not published; shop floor time and output capture - journals only, Insight Works Shop Floor Insight as low as $3.31 per shop floor employee per month; product configurator - Insight Works Product Configurator $276.00 per company per month; quality inspections - native Quality Management extension (2026), Insight Works Quality Inspector $184.00 per company per month; subcontracting - native; batch, co-products, catch weight - limited, Aptean apps price not published. What it costs: the Premium license is the only Microsoft price you can budget from a public page; implementation is quoted by partners. ERP Research estimates a typical three-year total cost of $75,000 to $400,000 and an implementation time of two to six months. Worked example (illustration): a 40-person metal fabricator with 9 office users and 25 people on the floor - licenses 9 x $110 x 12 = $11,880 per year; MxAPS at its starting price $170.20 x 12 = $2,042.40 per year; Product Configurator $276 x 12 = $3,312 per year; subtotal $17,234.40 per year in subscriptions before shop floor capture, Device licenses and implementation. Independent take: Business Central is a credible manufacturing ERP for discrete shops that want finance, inventory and production in one system with published pricing; the native BOM, routing and MRP features are mature, and the 2026 quality and subcontracting additions reduce the apps you need. Two points deserve attention: the Premium requirement applies to every full user in a manufacturing company, so price it across your whole user count; ask each partner to demonstrate your actual bottleneck schedule in the standard product and then with any proposed add-on. Three buyer scenarios: a 15-person kitting and light assembly business - start with Essentials and Assembly Management; a 60-person job shop with a bottleneck cell - Premium is required, budget for a finite scheduling app and a shop floor capture tool from the start; a 120-person food or chemical batch producer - batch sizing, co-products and catch weight usually call for an industry add-on such as Aptean's. When you do not need Business Central manufacturing: you may not need Premium if you resell finished goods, outsource all production, or assemble kits without routings; Essentials with purchasing, inventory and assembly covers that work at $30 less per user per month.
