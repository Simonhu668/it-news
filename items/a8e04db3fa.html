CISA、FBI、美国特勤局与德国、英国执法伙伴于 2026 年 10 月 20 日发布联合公告 AA26-293A，指出 SafePay 勒索团伙自 2024 年 8 月以来已攻陷至少 600 家关键基础设施与商业组织。团伙的初始入侵手段包括泄露或被盗凭证、钓鱼邮件、弱口令爆破，以及利用面向互联网设备的已知漏洞（公告列出 5 个 CVE，含 CVSS 9.8 的 CVE-2024-40711 与影响 SonicWall 的 CVE-2024-40766）。得手后，攻击者用 PsExec、WinRM、RDP 等合法管理工具横向移动，用 Mimikatz 窃取口令，用 WinSCP、RClone、7-Zip、WinRAR 等工具打包窃取数据。最值得注意的是其破坏恢复的手段：停掉 Windows 卷影复制服务，并把存放备份的 NAS 存储恢复出厂设置——调查中出现过受害方全部网络备份被删光的案例。之后文件被 ChaCha20+RSA 加密（扩展名 .SafePay），赎金通常按受害公司营收的一定比例以加密货币计价，拒付则数据被挂到泄露网站，团伙代表还会打电话施压。公告建议：优先修补暴露系统的已知可利用漏洞、强制多因素认证、限制管理权限、网段隔离；核心是保留攻击者够不着的离线、加密、不可变备份，并定期实测恢复流程。调查还观察到攻击者使用 Mullvad VPN 出口地址、被盗账户画像和自动化利用脚本；附带的 IOC（IP 地址）需结合上下文核验后再封堵。

原文

CISA has warned that SafePay ransomware actors have compromised at least 600 organizations across multiple critical infrastructure sectors, combining data theft and encryption with deliberate attacks on backup systems to obstruct recovery. The October 20, 2026, joint advisory (AA26-293A), published with the FBI, US Secret Service, and German and British law enforcement partners, describes operations spanning North America, South America, Europe, and Australia since August 2024. The advisory identifies five exploited vulnerabilities: CVE-2020-3259, CVE-2023-20269, CVE-2023-27532, CVE-2024-40711, and CVE-2024-40766. CVE-2024-40711 enables unauthenticated remote code execution through deserialization of untrusted data (CVSS 9.8). Once inside, attackers establish persistence via new or existing accounts, move laterally with PsExec, WinRM and RDP, and enumerate shares with ShareFinder.ps1 and SharpShares.exe. The recovery sabotage is particularly consequential: operators stop the Volume Shadow Copy Service and reset network attached storage containing backups to factory settings; investigators reported cases where all network-attached company backups were deleted. After exfiltration (WinSCP, RClone, OneDrive, Backblaze B2), files are encrypted with ChaCha20 and RSA, receiving the .SafePay extension; ransom notes point to a Tor chat portal and demands typically reflect a portion of company revenue in cryptocurrency. CISA urges organizations to prioritize exploited vulnerabilities on exposed systems, enforce MFA, restrict administrative privileges, segment networks, and maintain offline, encrypted, immutable backups with regularly tested restoration procedures. Defenders should investigate unexpected accounts, remote administration activity, security service changes, and unusual outbound transfers.
