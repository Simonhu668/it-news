汽车行业每天重复成千上万次操作，速度与精度决定表现。Infor CloudSuite Automotive 2026.10 版本带来面向行业的新自动化，目标是提升大批量运营中的合规性与效率；新预打包 AI 方案可让客户快速落地实用场景；模型上下文协议（MCP）的引入则简化了智能体企业的搭建与扩展，同时保持控制。供应商环节，标签与包装错误可能导致主机厂扣款、拒收或纠正措施要求。仓库人员现可使用移动扫描器，通过 Factory Track 生成客户专属标签：拣货完成时标签自动打印，基于扫描的核对确认正确的标签贴到正确的处理单元；这种防错做法在出库口发现贴错，而非在客户处发现。物流团队可在业务伙伴层面定义包装指令，预防包装错误；卡车装载优化也不再依赖人工计算外层处理单元，可直接在发运计划工作区完成。对主机厂，车辆识别号（VIN）登记现已原生内置于 ERP：可配置的 VIN 掩码按行业标准组织编号，强制 17 位格式、唯一性与校验位验证；在一个连续流程中，每辆车及其成本可立即追溯到单台，强化监管合规，省去单独 VIN 管理系统的成本与复杂性。质量管理团队关闭已明确处置的拒收检验单时，无须再走完整检验流程，减少了不必要的步骤并改善审计合规；隔离库存中的拒收数量更新后，ERP 会立即提醒物流与质量团队，避免物料滞留、占用库位并延误补货。Infor Velocity Suite 带来预打包行业 AI 方案，解决威胁交付表现的运营难题：提前发现缺料、发现新出现的质量问题、监控合同履约，从而更早采取行动，改善整体设备效率（OEE）、准时完整交付率（OTIF）与盈利能力。生产执行缺料检查方面，AI 智能体核对未来物料需求与预期库存并给出建议，例如从同一集团内邻近仓库调拨，或更新在缺料发生前到货的采购订单；计划人员保留控制权，可审阅建议、要求智能体执行变更并再次检查以确认缺料已解决。统计过程控制（SPC）与质量趋势检测方面，AI 监控生产测量数据，标记新出现的问题并评估缺陷风险，使工程师在纠正仍较简单时采取行动；清晰的质量图表与 AI 生成的摘要将针对每个公司与工作中心的洞察直接带入 Infor LN。合同履约组件方面，面向全球销售合同（动辄数十万行）的层级供应商，现可在发运计划与供应链经理工作区中，通过 AI 驱动的合同履约组件监控消耗情况，识别缺失消耗、偏差与需求模式，从而主动管理主机厂关系。从孤立 AI 试点扩展到覆盖业务的 AI 很快会变得复杂；2026.10 版本帮助消除这种复杂性，使汽车公司更容易将智能体 AI 扩展到计划、物流、质量、采购与核心流程。采用 MCP 后，基于 Infor LN 的 CloudSuite 方案为 AI 智能体提供了连接业务流程的标准方式，可访问 Infor LN 的 6,000 余个 OData 应用程序接口，无须硬编码集成。Infor 同时简化了智能体布局，将 100 余个单用途智能体合并为 12 个可通过生成式 AI 协同工作的领域智能体。自动化程度提高并不意味着放弃控制：现有 API 授权与安全策略继续约束访问，帮助组织在保持控制与可问责性的前提下扩展智能体 AI。每个版本中，Infor CloudSuite 都会增加面向行业的能力与基于标准功能的预打包行业 AI 方案，帮助组织在 30 天内解决痛点并见到成效（厂商自述）；前置部署工程师也会协助制定 AI 采纳路线图。

原文

In the automotive industry, performance depends on operations repeated thousands of times a day with speed and precision. With the 2026.10 release of Infor CloudSuite Automotive, Infor delivers new industry-specific automations that improve compliance and efficiency in high-volume operations. New pre-packaged artificial intelligence (AI) solutions enable customers to deploy practical use cases fast. Building and scaling the agentic enterprise while maintaining control is simplified with the new Model Context Protocol (MCP). For suppliers, labeling and packaging errors can incur original equipment manufacturer (OEM) chargeback, rejected shipments, or corrective action requests. Warehouse staff can now generate customer-specific labels with mobile scanners using Factory Track. When the box is picked, the label prints automatically and a scan-based check confirms the right label is applied to the right handling unit. This poka-yoke approach catches mislabeling at the dock, not at the customer. Logistics teams can prevent packaging errors by defining packaging instructions at the business partner level. Optimizing truck load has also become a lot easier without manual calculation of outer handling units, but directly in the shipment planning workspace. For OEMs, the vehicle identification number (VIN) registration is now built natively into the enterprise resource planning (ERP). A configurable VIN mask structures the number to industry standards, enforcing the 17-character format, uniqueness, and check-digit validation. In one continuous process, every vehicle and its costs are immediately traceable to the individual unit, strengthening regulatory compliance, and eliminating the cost and complexity of a separate VIN management system. Finally, we have made it easier for quality management teams to close rejected inspection orders. They no longer need to run the full inspection process where the disposition is already clear. That removes unnecessary steps and improves audit compliance. When a rejected quantity is updated in quarantine inventory, the ERP alerts logistics and quality teams straight away, so material doesn't sit unnoticed, blocking storage and delaying replenishment. Infor Velocity Suite brings pre-packaged industry AI solutions to solve operational challenges that put delivery performance at risk. The latest solutions anticipate material shortages, detect emerging quality issues, and monitor contract fulfilment, enabling earlier action to improve overall equipment effectiveness (OEE), OTIF, and profitability. Material shortage check for production execution: Protect assembly schedules by addressing material shortages before they disrupt production. An AI agent checks upcoming material needs against expected stock and recommends action, such as moving stock from a nearby warehouse in the same group or updating a purchase order due before the shortage occurs. Planners stay in control: they can review the recommendation, ask the agent to make the change, and run the check again to confirm that the shortage is resolved. SPC and quality trend detection: Reduce scrap and rework by spotting quality issues before they escalate. AI monitors production measurements, flags emerging problems, and assesses the risk of defects so engineers can act while corrections are still straightforward. Clear quality charts and an AI-generated summary bring tailored insights directly into Infor LN for each company and work center. Contract fulfilment widget: Tier suppliers running global sales contracts with hundreds of thousands of lines need to identify missing consumption, deviations, and demand patterns to manage the OEM relationship proactively. It is now possible for them to monitor consumption, via an AI-driven contract fulfilment widget directly in the shipping planner and supply chain manager workspaces. Moving from isolated AI pilots to AI that works across the business can quickly become complex. The 2026.10 release helps remove that complexity, making it easier for automotive companies to extend agentic AI across planning, logistics, quality, procurement, and other core processes. By adopting the Model Context Protocol, Infor LN-based CloudSuite solutions give AI agents a standard way to connect with business processes and access Infor LN's 6,000+ OData application programming interfaces (APIs), removing the need for hard-coded integrations. Infor has also simplified the agent landscape, consolidating more than 100 single-purpose agents into 12 domain agents that can work together through generative AI (GenAI). And greater automation does not have to mean giving up control. Existing API authorizations and security policies continue to govern access, helping organizations scale agentic AI across their landscape with control and accountability. With each release, Infor CloudSuite adds more industry-specific capabilities and pre-packaged industry AI solutions built on standard functionality, helping you solve pain points and deliver value in under 30 days.
