CISA 11 月 20 日再添 4 个在野利用缺陷：Cisco Expressway 硬编码凭据与 SAP NetWeaver 注入，11 月 23 日前须修复

正文 · AI 翻译

美国网络安全与基础设施安全局（CISA）于 2026 年 11 月 20 日向已知被利用漏洞（KEV）目录新增四个漏洞，其中最受关注的是 VMware vCenter 认证绕过缺陷 CVE-2026-123890，谷歌 Mandiant 称，一个与中国有关的间谍组织在 Broadcom 发布补丁之前已悄悄利用它超过两个月。联邦机构须在 11 月 23 日前修复全部四项缺陷，其余三项涉及 Cisco Expressway、SAP NetWeaver 与 Apache Roller——运行这些产品的任何组织都不应只把该期限当作政府要求，而应视为立即打补丁的信号。

KEV 目录回答的问题是：在每年发布的数万个 CVE 中，攻击者实际正在用哪些？根据第 22-01 号绑定操作指令，美国联邦机构须在目录所列截止日前修复每一条 KEV 条目，该目录也已成为全球私营部门安全团队事实上的补丁优先级清单。2026 年 11 月 20 日新增的四项为：CVE-2026-123890（VMware vCenter Server，认证绕过）；CVE-2026-76466（Cisco Expressway 系列，硬编码凭据）；CVE-2025-42884（SAP NetWeaver，JNDI 注入）；CVE-2026-129448（Apache Roller，未授权反序列化）。四项中三项为严重或接近严重级别，全部四项均已确认在野利用。

CVE-2026-123890 位于 vCenter 的目录服务（Directory Service），该组件支撑 vCenter 单点登录并保存整个环境的身份库。Broadcom 评为 CVSS 9.8 分：无需认证、无需用户交互，拥有 vCenter 网络访问权限的攻击者即可完全绕过登录、获得管理员级会话。Broadcom 于 2026 年 11 月 19 日通过 VMSA-2026-0009 公布缺陷并发布修复，CISA 次日将其列入 KEV。Mandiant 的时间线显示，该组织自 2026 年 9 月初起已把该缺陷作为未修复零日漏洞利用，距修复发布约两个半月；攻击者使用名为 BONESAW 的定制工具创建虚假 SSO 管理员账户并导出 vCenter 的目录数据库。修复版本为 vCenter 9.1.1.0200、9.0.2.0200 与 8.0 U3m，未打补丁的系统没有变通办法。

Cisco Expressway（CVE-2026-76466）：Cisco 于 2026 年 10 月 21 日披露该硬编码凭据缺陷，评为严重，CVSS 9.8 分。Expressway 是 Cisco 用于安全视频与语音协作的边缘网关，运行受影响代码的 Expressway-C 与 Expressway-E 集群部署可让未授权攻击者以 root 执行命令。Cisco 称没有变通办法，唯一修复是升级到 X15.5.20 或更高版本；较旧的 X14 系统没有补丁，须完整迁移。

SAP NetWeaver（CVE-2025-42884）：本批中最旧的缺陷，SAP 已于 2025 年 11 月通过 SAP Security Note 3660969 发布补丁，距其列入 KEV 超过一年。它是 SAP NetWeaver Enterprise Portal 中的 JNDI 注入漏洞，可让远程已认证攻击者执行任意代码。补丁与列入 KEV 之间间隔漫长，恰恰说明 CISA 一旦确认存在主动利用就会列入 CVE——哪怕是一年前的缺陷；这通常意味着未打补丁的 NetWeaver Enterprise Portal 实例至今仍在被攻击。

Apache Roller（CVE-2026-129448）：Apache 软件基金会于 2026 年 11 月 18 日披露，Roller 的 XML-RPC 端点存在未授权反序列化缺陷，CVSS 9.8 分，已确认在野利用，攻击者无需登录即可执行任意代码。影响 Roller 4.0.0 至 6.1.4（相关 CVE-2026-82384 覆盖 6.1.5）。Apache 称，仅在管理控制台禁用 XML-RPC 无济于事，因为有缺陷的反序列化发生在该设置被检查之前。真正的修复是升级到 Roller 6.1.6 或更高版本；无法立即升级的组织须编辑 WEB-INF/web.xml 移除 XML-RPC servlet 映射并重启应用。

一个常见误解是 KEV 目录是未修复缺陷的清单。事实并非如此——包括本批四项中的三项在内，大多数 KEV 条目在列入之前已有厂商修复可用。KEV 状态确认的是真实攻击者正在对真实受害者使用该缺陷，而非实验室里的理论验证。SAP NetWeaver 条目是最清晰的例子：它已打补丁超过一年，直到 CISA 确认仍在持续利用才列入，这表明未打补丁的实例至今仍是现实目标。

核查与修复方法：vCenter——对照 Broadcom 的 VMSA-2026-0009 公告核对构建号，升级到 9.1.1.0200、9.0.2.0200 或 8.0 U3m；没有变通办法，无法立即打补丁时应限制 vCenter 管理接口的网络访问，并检查目录服务日志中是否存在非预期的 SSO 管理员账户创建。Cisco Expressway——确认部署是否为集群（主要风险路径），将 Expressway-C/E 升级到 X15.5.20 或更高版本；X14 部署没有修复，须迁移到受支持版本。SAP NetWeaver——对尚未打补丁的 NetWeaver Enterprise Portal 系统（EP-BASIS/EP-RUNTIME 7.50）应用 SAP Security Note 3660969，并按 SAP 建议检查 JNDI 相关配置。Apache Roller——升级到 6.1.6 或更高版本；无法立即升级时，从 WEB-INF/web.xml 中移除 XML-RPC servlet 映射并重启，仅通过管理界面关闭 XML-RPC 并不能堵住该缺陷。

原文

The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency added four vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog on November 20, 2026, and the headline entry is a VMware vCenter authentication bypass, CVE-2026-123890, that Google's Mandiant says a China-linked espionage group quietly exploited for more than two months before Broadcom ever shipped a patch. Federal civilian agencies have until November 23 to remediate all four flaws, which also include bugs in Cisco Expressway, SAP NetWeaver and Apache Roller — but any organization running these products, not just U.S. government networks, should treat the deadline as a patch-now signal.

CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog exists to answer one question for defenders: out of the tens of thousands of CVEs published every year, which ones are attackers actually using right now? Binding Operational Directive 22-01 requires U.S. federal civilian agencies to patch any KEV entry by its listed due date, and the catalog has become the de facto patch-priority list for private-sector security teams worldwide, too. On November 20, 2026, CISA added four new entries, all due by November 23: CVE-2026-123890 — VMware vCenter Server, authentication bypass using an alternate path or channel; CVE-2026-76466 — Cisco Expressway Series, use of hard-coded credentials; CVE-2025-42884 — SAP NetWeaver, improper neutralization of special elements in data query logic (JNDI injection); CVE-2026-129448 — Apache Roller, deserialization of untrusted data. Three of the four are rated critical or near-critical, and all four are now confirmed by CISA to have been exploited in the wild.

CVE-2026-123890 sits in vCenter's Directory Service, the component that backs vCenter Single Sign-On and holds the identity store for the whole environment. Broadcom rates it CVSS 9.8 out of 10 — because it requires no authentication and no user interaction: an attacker with network access to vCenter can bypass login entirely and land an administrator-level session. Broadcom disclosed the flaw and shipped fixes on November 19, 2026 through advisory VMSA-2026-0009, one day before CISA added it to the KEV catalog. Google's Mandiant threat intelligence team has laid out that the China-linked group it tracks had already been exploiting CVE-2026-123890 as an unpatched zero-day since roughly early September 2026. Mandiant's reporting describes the attackers using a custom post-exploitation tool it calls BONESAW to create a rogue SSO administrator account and exfiltrate vCenter's directory database. Broadcom patched it on Nov. 19, 2026 via VMSA-2026-0009; fixed builds are vCenter 9.1.1.0200, 9.0.2.0200 and 8.0 U3m, with no workaround for unpatched systems.

Cisco Expressway (CVE-2026-76466): Cisco disclosed this hard-coded-credentials flaw on October 21, 2026, rating it critical with a CVSS score of 9.8. Expressway is Cisco's edge gateway for secure video and voice collaboration, and clustered Expressway-C and Expressway-E deployments running affected code let an unauthenticated attacker execute commands as root. Cisco says there's no workaround — the only fix is upgrading to X15.5.20 or later, with older X14 systems needing a full migration since no patch exists for that branch.

SAP NetWeaver (CVE-2025-42884): This is the oldest flaw in the batch, originally patched by SAP in November 2025 via SAP Security Note 3660969, more than a year before its KEV addition. It's a JNDI injection vulnerability in SAP NetWeaver Enterprise Portal that can let a remote, authenticated attacker run arbitrary code. Its long gap between patch and KEV listing is a reminder that CISA adds a CVE whenever it confirms active exploitation — even a year-old bug earns a spot the moment evidence of real-world attacks surfaces, which typically means unpatched NetWeaver Enterprise Portal instances are still being targeted today.

Apache Roller (CVE-2026-129448): Roller is an open-source, Java-based blogging platform. The Apache Software Foundation disclosed on November 18, 2026 that this unauthenticated deserialization flaw in Roller's XML-RPC endpoint — rated CVSS 9.8 — was under active exploitation, letting an attacker run arbitrary code without logging in first. It affects Roller 4.0.0 through 6.1.4 (a related CVE, CVE-2026-82384, covers 6.1.5), and Apache says simply disabling XML-RPC in the admin console doesn't help, because the vulnerable deserialization happens before that setting is even checked. The real fix is upgrading to Roller 6.1.6 or later; organizations that can't patch immediately need to edit WEB-INF/web.xml to remove the XML-RPC servlet mapping and restart the application.

A common misunderstanding is that CISA's KEV catalog is a list of unpatched bugs. It isn't — most KEV entries, including three of these four, already had vendor fixes available before CISA added them. KEV status is instead a confirmation that real attackers, not just researchers in a lab, are actively using the flaw against real victims. The SAP NetWeaver entry on this list, patched over a year ago, is the clearest example: it sat unlisted until CISA confirmed ongoing exploitation, which is the signal that unpatched NetWeaver Enterprise Portal instances are still a live target today, not a historical footnote.

How to check exposure and patch each product: VMware vCenter — check your build number against Broadcom's VMSA-2026-0009 advisory and update to 9.1.1.0200, 9.0.2.0200 or 8.0 U3m. There is no workaround — if you can't patch immediately, restrict network access to the vCenter management interface as a stopgap, and review Directory Service logs for unexpected SSO administrator account creation. Cisco Expressway — confirm whether your deployment is clustered (the primary risk path) and upgrade Expressway-C/E to X15.5.20 or later. X14 deployments have no fix and need to migrate to a supported release. SAP NetWeaver — apply SAP Security Note 3660969 on any NetWeaver Enterprise Portal system (EP-BASIS/EP-RUNTIME 7.50) that hasn't already been patched, and review JNDI-related configuration as SAP recommends. Apache Roller — upgrade to 6.1.6 or later. If an immediate upgrade isn't possible, remove the XML-RPC servlet mapping from WEB-INF/web.xml and restart — disabling XML-RPC through the admin UI alone does not close the hole.
