Teams 11 月三项带日期的变化：PowerPoint Live 翻译 11 月 30 日退役，移动端 12 月中旬起须 iOS 18

Seton Hall 大学 IT 部门发布了 2026 年 11 月 Microsoft 365 汇总，涉及六项变化：Teams 分组讨论室扩容、OneDrive 按钮移位、一项 PowerPoint Live 功能退役、Teams 新增钓鱼防护、iPhone 与 iPad 提出新系统要求，以及语音邮件通知的变化。各项变化落地日期不同，以下按时间分别说明。

六项变化一览：分组讨论室支持最多 1000 人、100 间（Teams，现已可用，会议组织者）；“新建或上传”按钮移位（OneDrive 网页版，2026 年 10 月下旬至 12 月初推送，服务台与培训人员）；翻译幻灯片退役（Teams 中的 PowerPoint Live，2026 年 11 月 30 日，多语言演示者）；AI 钓鱼检测（Teams，预计 2026 年 12 月初至中旬全面可用，无需管理员操作）；须 iOS 18 或 iPadOS 18（Teams 移动端，2026 年 12 月中旬至 2027 年 1 月初，设备与 MDM 管理员）；语音邮件通知发件人变化（Teams 电话，2027 年 3 月起，邮件流管理员）。

PowerPoint Live 幻灯片翻译于 11 月 30 日结束：Seton Hall 称，PowerPoint Live 中的“翻译幻灯片”功能将于 11 月 30 日退役。此后参会者在 Teams 中查看幻灯片时无法再将其翻译成其他语言，幻灯片只显示原文。消息中心公告 MC1532259 与此一致：参会者将不再看到“更改视图 > 翻译幻灯片”。微软称，使用 PowerPoint Live 演示及其他参会者查看选项不受影响，且没有可管理此变化的管理员设置。微软同时表示，很少有组织会注意到，因为只有极小比例的 PowerPoint Live 会话使用该功能。微软建议演示者提前按受众所需语言准备幻灯片，提到该功能的培训内容应更新。此次退役仅涉及 PowerPoint Live 中的参会者幻灯片翻译，不涉及其他 PowerPoint 翻译工具。

Teams 新增 AI 钓鱼检测：Teams 将新增钓鱼检测，依据对话上下文而非仅链接与附件进行判断。据 BleepingComputer 转述的微软消息中心措辞，该功能用于识别钓鱼、社交工程、冒充与欺骗意图；即使消息中没有恶意链接或文件，也能标记可疑行为。首批重点是高风险对话，主要是与外部联系人的首次对话。隐私方面，微软称该功能仅用于安全，不用于摘要、广告或用户监控；检测系统不会保留消息内容用于模型训练或其他次要用途。管理员无需开启。时间安排：定向发布已于 2026 年 11 月初开始，全球全面可用预计在 12 月初至中旬。这针对的是真实缺口：许多 Teams 诈骗根本不含恶意链接，攻击者冒充服务台或高管、要求对方执行操作；看上下文的检测能抓住其中一部分，但不是全部，仍须培训用户核实来自外部联系人的意外请求。

iPhone 与 iPad 上的 Teams 将要求 iOS 18：微软 Teams 将停止对 iOS 17 与 iPadOS 17 的新版本支持。变化推送后，新版 Teams 在 iPhone 上要求 iOS 18 或更高版本，在 iPad 上要求 iPadOS 18 或更高版本。公告 MC1556088 给出时间表：2026 年 12 月中旬开始推送，预计 2027 年 1 月初完成。旧设备上的影响：Teams 不会停止工作，已安装的版本可继续使用，只是不再获得官方支持；也不会阻止打开或登录现有安装。只是这些设备收不到新的 Teams 更新，包括未来的安全修复。用户可在“设置 > 通用 > 关于本机 > 软件版本”查看版本，先升级 iOS 或 iPadOS，再从 App Store 更新 Teams。管理员方面，微软建议找出仍在 iOS 17 或 iPadOS 17 上的设备并通知相关用户，同时检查固定或要求特定 iOS/iPadOS 版本的 MDM 或应用部署策略，更新为允许 iOS 18/iPadOS 18，以免受影响设备无法升级；Intune 及其他 MDM 管理员应在 12 月中旬前核查系统版本策略。背景：这符合微软长期做法——微软在问答中称，iOS 支持仅限最近的两个主要版本。

Teams 语音邮件通知 2027 年 3 月起更换发件人：自 2027 年 3 月起，语音邮件通知邮件将以“Microsoft Teams”作为发件人，而非来电者姓名或电话号码；来电者信息将出现在主题行与正文内。其目标是提升邮件安全、降低冒充风险。语音邮件通知仍会送达，Teams 内的来电者信息不变。代价是部分系统可能依赖现有发件人：3 月之前应检查按发件人分类或转发语音邮件的 Outlook 规则、匹配现有发件人的邮件流规则与允许列表，以及解析语音邮件的自动化或工单设置。习惯按发件人姓名搜索收件箱的用户，之后须改按主题行搜索。微软的时间表仍可能变动，须关注消息中心更新。

现在该做什么：11 月 30 日之前，告知多语言演示者翻译功能即将取消并备好已翻译的讲稿，更新 OneDrive 网页版培训截图与服务台脚本；12 月中旬之前，找出仍在 iOS 17 或 iPadOS 17 上的 iPhone 与 iPad，确认 MDM 策略允许升级到 iOS 18 与 iPadOS 18，并提醒员工 Teams 钓鱼检测只是补充防护、不能替代自身谨慎；2027 年 3 月之前，审计依赖现有语音邮件发件人的邮件规则与自动化。

原文

Seton Hall University's IT division published its November 2026 Microsoft 365 roundup with six changes. Teams breakout rooms get bigger, a OneDrive button moves, one PowerPoint Live feature is retired, and Teams gets new phishing protection, new iPhone and iPad requirements and a change to voicemail emails. The changes land on different dates, so the details below separate what is already here from what is still on the way.

The six changes at a glance: breakout rooms for up to 1,000 people in 100 rooms (Teams, available now, meeting organizers); Create or upload button moves (OneDrive for the web, rolling out from late October to early December 2026, help desks and trainers); Translate slides retired (PowerPoint Live in Teams, November 30, 2026, presenters with multilingual audiences); AI phishing detection (Teams, general availability expected early to mid December 2026, no admin action required); iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 required (Teams mobile, mid-December 2026 to early January 2027, device and MDM admins); voicemail email sender changes (Teams Calling, from March 2027, mail-flow admins).

PowerPoint Live slide translation ends November 30: Seton Hall reports that the Translate slides feature in PowerPoint Live is being retired on November 30. After that, attendees can't translate slides into another language while viewing them in Teams. Slides will show in their original language. Message Center notice MC1532259 matches this. Attendees will no longer find Change view > Translate slides. Microsoft says presenting with PowerPoint Live and other attendee viewing options are unaffected. There is no admin setting to manage the change. Microsoft also says few organizations will notice, because only a very small share of PowerPoint Live sessions use the option. Microsoft recommends that presenters share slides in the languages their audience needs ahead of time. Training content that mentions Translate slides should be updated. This retirement covers attendee slide translation in PowerPoint Live only; it does not cover other PowerPoint translation tools.

Teams adds AI phishing detection: Teams is getting phishing detection that looks at the context of a conversation, not just links and attachments. According to Microsoft's Message Center wording, as reported by BleepingComputer, it is designed to spot phishing, social engineering, impersonation attempts and deceptive intent. It can flag suspicious behavior even when a message has no malicious link or file. The first focus is higher-risk chats, mainly the first conversation with an external contact. On privacy, Microsoft says the feature is used only for security, not for summarization, advertising or user monitoring. It also says the detection system does not keep message content for model training or other secondary uses. Admins don't need to turn anything on. Timing: targeted release began in early November 2026, and worldwide general availability is expected between early and mid-December. This targets a real gap: many Teams scams don't contain a malicious link at all. The attacker poses as help desk staff or an executive and asks the target to do something. Detection that looks at context can catch some of these, but it won't catch every scam. Keep training users to verify unexpected requests from external contacts.

Teams on iPhone and iPad will require iOS 18: Microsoft Teams is ending support for upcoming builds on iOS 17 and iPadOS 17. As this change rolls out, new Teams releases will require iOS 18 or later on iPhone and iPadOS 18 or later on iPad. Notice MC1556088 sets the schedule: rollout begins in mid-December 2026 and is expected to complete by early January 2027. What happens to older devices: Teams doesn't stop working. Teams versions already installed on these devices will continue functioning without official support. This change does not introduce a block on opening or signing in to those existing installations. Those devices just won't get new Teams updates, including future security fixes. For users: check your version in Settings > General > About > Software Version. Update iOS or iPadOS, then update Teams from the Apple App Store. For admins: Microsoft recommends that you identify devices running iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 and notify affected users. Then review any mobile device management (MDM) or app deployment policies that pin or require specific iOS/iPadOS versions, and update them to allow iOS 18/iPadOS 18 so affected devices are not blocked from upgrading. Intune and other MDM admins should check OS version policies before mid-December. Background: this follows Microsoft's long-standing approach. In a Microsoft Q&A answer, Microsoft says that for iOS, support is limited to the two most recent major versions.

Teams voicemail emails get a new sender, starting March 2027: from March 2027, voicemail notification emails will show Microsoft Teams as the sender instead of the caller's name or phone number. Caller details will appear in the subject line and message body. The stated goal is better email security and less risk of impersonation. Voicemail notifications still arrive, and caller information inside Teams doesn't change. The trade-off is that some systems may depend on the current sender. Before March, check Outlook rules that sort or forward voicemail emails by sender, mail-flow rules and allow lists that match the current sender, and any automation or ticketing setups that parse voicemail emails. Users who search their inbox by the caller's name in the From field will need to search the subject line instead.

What to do now. Before November 30: tell presenters with multilingual audiences that slide translation is going away, and have translated decks ready; update OneDrive web training screenshots and help desk scripts. Before mid-December: find iPhones and iPads still on iOS 17 or iPadOS 17; check that MDM policies allow upgrades to iOS 18 and iPadOS 18; remind staff that Teams phishing detection adds protection but doesn't replace their own caution. Before March 2027: audit mail rules and automation that depend on the current voicemail sender.
