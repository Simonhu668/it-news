💡 每日小技巧 · 10月29日

---

1️⃣ EBS：报表跑失败了别干着急，点 View Log 看报错原因

请求状态是 Error 或 Warning 时，报错原因就藏在日志里：

  1. 打开“查看我的请求”（View Requests），找到状态为 Error 的请求。
  2. 选中该行，点 View Log（查看日志）。
  3. 拉到日志末尾找 error / ORA- 开头的行，那就是失败原因（如参数填错、数据不存在）。
  4. 把这行报错发给 IT 或按提示修正参数后重新提交，比只说“报表跑不出来”快得多。

English — EBS: If your report fails, click View Log to find out why

  1. Open View Requests and find your request with status Error.
  2. Select it and click View Log.
  3. Scroll to the end of the log and look for lines starting with error or ORA- — that is the failure reason (e.g. a wrong parameter or missing data).
  4. Send that line to IT, or fix the parameter and resubmit — much faster than just saying "the report didn't run."

---

2️⃣ Teams：开会共享时只共享单个窗口，别共享整个桌面

共享整个屏幕时，微信、邮件弹窗都可能被同事看到，选单个窗口更稳妥：

  1. 会议中点 共享（Share），选“窗口（Window）”而不是“屏幕（Screen）”。
  2. 在列表里点你要展示的那一个窗口（如 PPT 或 Excel）。
  3. 共享期间切换到别的窗口时，对方画面不会跟着变，隐私不外泄。
  4. 讲完点 停止共享（Stop sharing） 即可。

English — Teams: Share one window in a meeting, not your whole desktop

  1. In the meeting, click Share and choose "Window" instead of "Screen".
  2. Pick the single window you want to show (e.g. your PPT or Excel file).
  3. While sharing, switching to another window won't change what others see — your pop-ups stay private.
  4. Click Stop sharing when you are done.

---

3️⃣ OneDrive / SharePoint：共享链接设个有效期，到期自动失效

临时共享的文件不用事后逐个收回权限，设好日期让它自动过期：

  1. 选中文件点 共享（Share），在链接设置里点齿轮图标（链接设置）。
  2. 找到 设置过期日期（Set expiration date），选一个截止日期（如项目结束那天）。
  3. 按需再设为“仅查看”，点应用并复制链接发出。
  4. 到期后链接自动失效，对方再打不开，不用你手动取消共享。

English — OneDrive / SharePoint: Set an expiration date on a shared link so it stops working by itself

  1. Select the file, click Share, then the gear icon (link settings).
  2. Turn on Set expiration date and pick a cut-off date (e.g. the project end date).
  3. Set it to view-only if needed, apply, and copy the link to send.
  4. After that date the link stops working on its own — no need to revoke access manually.

1️⃣ EBS：报表跑完别只看状态，点 View Output 直接看结果

跑报表时只看“已完成”还不够——结果文件要在这里打开：

  1. 打开“查看我的请求”（View Requests），找到状态为 Completed 的请求。
  2. 选中该行，点 View Output（查看输出）。
  3. 结果会在浏览器中直接打开（PDF / Excel / 文本），不用等邮件、不用再去别处找文件。
  4. 需要保存时，在打开的页面里点下载或打印即可。

English — EBS: After your report finishes, click View Output to see the result

  1. Open View Requests and find your request with status Completed.
  2. Select it and click View Output.
  3. The result opens in your browser (PDF / Excel / text) — no need to wait for an email.
  4. Download or print it from there if you need to keep it.

---

2️⃣ Outlook：把邮件拖到“任务”图标，一键变成待办

需要跟进的邮件别只留在收件箱，拖一下就变成任务：

  1. 在 Outlook 左下角找到 任务（To Do） 图标。
  2. 把需要跟进的邮件直接拖到该图标上松开。
  3. 自动生成一条带原邮件内容的任务，补上截止日期即可。
  4. 到期前它会提醒你，再也不怕邮件沉底忘跟进。

English — Outlook: Drag an email onto the Tasks icon to turn it into a to-do

  1. Find the Tasks (To Do) icon at the bottom left of Outlook.
  2. Drag the email you need to follow up onto that icon and release.
  3. A task is created with the original email attached — just add a due date.
  4. It will remind you before the deadline, so nothing sinks to the bottom of your inbox.

---

3️⃣ Excel：Ctrl + 方向键，一秒跳到数据区域的边缘

长表格不用一页页滚，用键盘直接跳：

  1. 点进数据区域的任意单元格。
  2. 按 Ctrl + ↓ 跳到本列最后一个有数据的单元格；Ctrl + ↑ 回到顶部。
  3. Ctrl + → / ← 同理，横向跳到本行数据的左右边缘。
  4. 配合 Shift 使用（如 Ctrl + Shift + ↓）可一键选中整段数据，方便复制或套格式。

English — Excel: Ctrl + arrow keys jump to the edge of your data in one second

  1. Click any cell inside your data range.
  2. Press Ctrl + ↓ to jump to the last filled cell in the column; Ctrl + ↑ to go back to the top.
  3. Ctrl + → / ← do the same sideways, to the left/right edge of the row's data.
  4. Add Shift (e.g. Ctrl + Shift + ↓) to select the whole block at once for copying or formatting.

1️⃣ EBS 改完数据别直接关窗口？先按 Ctrl+S 保存

📌 在 EBS 里改了字段，不点保存就关窗口，改动可能没写进系统，白改一场。

① 改完字段后按 Ctrl+S（或点工具栏的保存图标）保存
② 保存成功后，数据才真正写进系统
③ 如果直接关窗口，系统会弹提示问是否保存——选“保存”才不会丢改动

💡 必填项没填会报错，补上再按一次 Ctrl+S 就好。

1️⃣ Changed data in EBS? Save with Ctrl+S before closing the window

① After editing a field, press Ctrl+S (or click the Save icon in the toolbar)
② Only after saving is the change actually written to the system
③ If you close the window directly, the system will ask whether to save — choose “Save” so your changes are not lost

---

2️⃣ OneDrive 文件误删了？先去回收站找回来，不用重做

📌 手滑删了文件别慌，也别急着重做一份，先去回收站看看。

① 打开 OneDrive（网页版或电脑上的 OneDrive），进入 回收站（Recycle bin）
② 找到误删的文件，选中后点 还原（Restore），文件就回到原来的文件夹
③ 回原位置确认一下文件回来了，再继续用

💡 发现误删越早去回收站越好，养成先找回收站再重做的习惯。

2️⃣ Deleted a OneDrive file by mistake? Restore it from the Recycle Bin

① Open OneDrive (on the web or on your computer) and go to the Recycle Bin
② Find the deleted file, select it and click Restore — it goes back to its original folder
③ Check the original location to confirm it is back before continuing

---

3️⃣ Windows 想快速打开文件夹？按 Win+E，不用先找“此电脑”

📌 要找文件时不用先在桌面找图标，一个快捷键直接打开资源管理器。

① 按住 Win 键再按 E，文件资源管理器立刻打开
② 在左侧点常用文件夹，或在地址栏输入路径，直接跳过去
③ 需要对照两个文件夹时，再按一次 Win+E 又开一个窗口，方便两边对照着拖文件

💡 找文件先按 Win+E，少点好几下鼠标。

3️⃣ Want to open a folder fast in Windows? Press Win+E, no need to find “This PC”

① Hold the Win key and press E — File Explorer opens right away
② Click a frequent folder on the left, or type a path in the address bar to jump there
③ To compare two folders, press Win+E again for a second window and drag files between them

1️⃣ EBS 查数据不用死记编号？用 > < 直接筛范围

📌 想找“数量大于 100 的单据”或“某天之后的订单”，不用先导出再慢慢翻。

① 按 F11 进入查询模式
② 在数字或日期字段里直接输入比较符，例如 >100（大于 100）、>=2026-10-01（某天及之后）
③ 按 Ctrl+F11 执行查询，只返回符合条件的记录

💡 >、<、>=、<= 都能这样用，比逐条翻快得多。

1️⃣ No need to remember exact numbers in EBS? Filter with > and <

① Press F11 to enter query mode
② In a number or date field, type a comparison, e.g. >100 (more than 100) or >=2026-10-01 (on or after that date)
③ Press Ctrl+F11 to run the query — only matching records come back

---

2️⃣ Outlook 给邮件贴个颜色标签，重要邮件一眼找到

📌 每天邮件一多，项目邮件、审批邮件全混在一起，颜色标签能帮你一眼区分。

① 选中一封邮件，点顶部菜单的 分类（Categorize）
② 选一个颜色（比如项目 A 用红色、审批用黄色），邮件前面就会带上颜色标记
③ 想取消时，再点 分类 → 清除所有类别 即可

💡 给固定项目统一用一个颜色，扫一眼收件箱就知道哪封归哪类。

2️⃣ Tag Outlook mail with a color category to spot important mail at a glance

① Select a message and click Categorize in the top menu
② Pick a color (e.g. red for Project A, yellow for approvals) — a color tag appears next to the message
③ To remove it, click Categorize → Clear All Categories

---

3️⃣ Teams 开会想发言？点「举手」，不用打断别人

📌 开会时想插话又不想打断正在讲的人，先举手等主持人点你。

① 会议工具栏点 举手（Raise hand） 图标，你的名字旁会出现举手标志
② 主持人看到后会请你发言；说完再点一次举手图标，把手放下
③ 快捷键 Ctrl+Shift+K 也能开关举手，不用找按钮

💡 人多的会先举手再发言，会议秩序好很多。

3️⃣ Want to speak in a Teams meeting? Raise your hand instead of interrupting

① In the meeting toolbar, click the Raise hand icon — a hand appears next to your name
② The host will invite you to speak; when you finish, click it again to lower your hand
③ Shortcut Ctrl+Shift+K toggles raise hand too, no need to find the button

③ Or just click the mic icon in the meeting bar — same result

📌 地址、备注太长挤在一行看不清？不用拉宽列，也不用拆成两个格子。

① 双击单元格进入编辑状态，把光标放在要换行的位置
② 按 Alt+Enter，光标后面的内容就换到下一行
③ 按 Enter 确认，行高会自动撑开

💡 写地址（省市区一行、街道门牌一行）时最常用。

3️⃣ Need two lines in one Excel cell? Press Alt+Enter

① Double-click the cell to edit it, and place your cursor where the break should go
② Press Alt+Enter — everything after the cursor moves to a new line in the same cell
③ Press Enter to confirm; the row height adjusts automatically

① EBS 审批单据不用进系统：审批通知邮件里直接回复 Approve 就行

  • 收到 EBS 发来的审批通知邮件（比如请购单、费用报销待你审批）
  • 按邮件里的提示，直接回复邮件正文写 Approve（批准）或 Reject（拒绝）
  • 点发送后系统自动更新审批状态，不用再登录 EBS 进工作清单点审批

📌 English: EBS: approve from your email — no need to log in

  • Got an EBS approval notification email (e.g. a purchase requisition or expense claim waiting for you)?
  • Reply to it with just "Approve" (or "Reject") in the email body, as the message instructs
  • Send, and the approval status updates automatically — no need to log in to EBS and open the worklist

② Excel 输入今天日期不用翻日历：Ctrl+; 一键填入，Ctrl+Shift+; 填当前时间

  • 在单元格里按 Ctrl+;（分号），立刻填入今天的日期 —— 写日报、登记表时最省事
  • 按 Ctrl+Shift+;，填入当前时间（几点几分）
  • 两个一起按就是日期+时间戳；注意这是固定值，之后不会跟着日期自动变

📌 English: Excel: Ctrl+; inserts today's date, Ctrl+Shift+; inserts the current time

  • In a cell, press Ctrl+; (semicolon) to drop in today's date instantly — perfect for logs and daily reports
  • Press Ctrl+Shift+; to insert the current time
  • They insert fixed values, not formulas, so they won't change when the date rolls over

③ 离开工位先锁屏：按 Win+L，一秒保护电脑

  • 去开会、接水、吃午饭前，按 Win+L（L = Lock），电脑立刻锁定
  • 回来输入密码/PIN 就能继续，刚才的工作原样不动
  • 养成习惯：人走屏锁，别人路过也看不到你的邮件和单据

📌 English: Windows: press Win+L to lock your PC before you walk away

  • Before a meeting, a coffee run, or lunch, press Win+L (L = Lock) to lock your computer instantly
  • Sign back in with your password or PIN and everything is exactly where you left it
  • Make it a habit: when you leave, lock the screen — passers-by can't peek at your mail or documents

📌 English: Windows: press Win+D to peek at the desktop, press again to restore

  • With a dozen windows open, press Win+D (D = Desktop) to minimize them all at once
  • Press Win+D again and every window comes back exactly as it was
  • Want minimize-only? Use Win+M; Win+Shift+M brings the minimized windows back

  1. Home tab → Quick Steps group → click "Create New" Quick Step;
  2. Choose "Move to folder", give it a name (e.g., "To Do"), pick the target folder, and assign a shortcut (e.g., Ctrl+Shift+1);
  3. Select an email and hit the shortcut to file it instantly — much faster than drag-and-drop.
